Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Your data

ChordGrid does not collect, track, sell, or transmit personal data to the developer.

Local storage

Projects (including their keys and chord symbols), personal chords, and preferences are stored locally on your device and remain there until you delete them or remove the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete projects and personal chords inside ChordGrid at any time, or delete the app to remove all of its data. Because no data is sent to the developer, there is nothing to request or revoke. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, those backups may include ChordGrid’s data until they are deleted.

Purchases

ChordGrid uses Apple’s StoreKit to process purchases. Apple handles purchase and account information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Third parties

ChordGrid contains no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs, and it does not share data with third parties. The only outside service it uses is Apple’s App Store for purchases.

Exports and sharing

Exported images, PDF files, and editable .chordgrid project files leave the app only when you choose to export or share them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to dzmitry.kurski.support@gmail.com.
