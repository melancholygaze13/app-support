ChordGrid

Create clear chord diagrams and assemble them into printable or shareable sheets.

Version 1.0iOS & iPadOS 26+

Create chords quickly

Build six-string guitar and four-string bass diagrams with fretted notes, open and muted strings, capo position, starting fret, and custom tuning.

Build complete sheets

Queue multiple chords, reorder them, preview the full sheet, and save projects automatically. ChordGrid supports undo and redo throughout editing.

Export your work

Export chord sheets as PNG files. ChordGrid Pro also adds PDF export, additional layouts and string counts, custom themes, fingering numbers, and watermark controls.

Privacy by default

Projects and preferences stay on your device. ChordGrid does not collect, track, sell, or transmit personal data to the developer.
