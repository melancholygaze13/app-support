ChordGrid

Create precise guitar and bass chords, reuse shapes from your Chord Library, arrange them into clear sheets, and share your work.

Version 1.1.0iOS & iPadOS 26+

What’s new in 1.1.0

The Chord Library and editable project sharing make it easier to reuse chords and keep working across projects. This release also improves barre and fingering-number rendering, Chord Library controls at larger text sizes, and the reliability of saving personal chords.

Create precise chord diagrams

Choose guitar or bass, place fretted notes, and mark open or muted strings. Set the starting fret and capo position, customize the tuning across your sheet, name chords, and use undo and redo as you work.

Keep your chords close

Start with built-in presets for common six-string guitar chords, or save personal chords to reuse across projects. Search by name, browse favorites and recently used chords, and filter by instrument, string count, tuning, and capo. Load a chord for editing or quickly add it to your sheet.

Build organized chord sheets

Add multiple diagrams, reorder them with drag and drop or menu actions, and reopen any chord for editing. Preview the completed sheet before exporting, choose a light or dark appearance, and rely on automatic project saving.

Share editable projects

Export complete projects as .chordgrid files and share them with other ChordGrid users to continue editing. Import through Files or open projects from other apps. Keep both copies, or replace an existing project while retaining a recovery copy. For finished sheets, export PNG images or PDF files with Pro.

Free features

The free version includes six-string guitar and four-string bass diagrams, the Chord Library, editable project import and export, Grid sheet layouts, and PNG export with a “Made with ChordGrid” watermark.

ChordGrid Pro

One optional, permanent purchase unlocks:

  • 7- and 8-string guitar diagrams
  • 5- and 6-string bass diagrams
  • Row and Column sheet layouts in addition to Grid
  • Custom sheet colors, typography, and transparency
  • Custom watermark text or watermark removal
  • Numbered fingering on chord diagrams
  • PDF export

Privacy by default

No account is required, and ChordGrid does not use analytics or tracking. Your projects, personal chords, and preferences are stored on your device. You choose what to export and share.

Available in eight languages

Use ChordGrid in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Russian, or Simplified Chinese.
